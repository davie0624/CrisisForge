## U.S. Treasury Daily Par Yield Curve Rates

Source: Office of Debt Management, U.S. Department of the Treasury, **Daily
Treasury Par Yield Curve Rates**. The official Data.gov record marks this dataset
public and releases it under **CC0 1.0**. CrisisForge's duration-convexity return
proxies are independent derived estimates; they are not official Treasury
total-return series and are not endorsed by the U.S. Department of the Treasury.

- Official catalog and license: <https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/interest-rate-statistics-daily-treasury-yield-curve-rates>
- CC0 1.0: <https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>
- Current Treasury page: <https://home.treasury.gov/resource-center/data-chart-center/interest-rates/TextView>
- Official XML feed documentation: <https://home.treasury.gov/treasury-daily-interest-rate-xml-feed>
